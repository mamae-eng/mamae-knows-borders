Bulacan to Global Borders

Hi, I'm Mamae!

I grew up in a small town in Bulacan, Philippines dreaming of Middle Earth and stamping my passport until it begged for mercy. At 20 years old, I survived the legendary 3:00 AM Manila stampede just to process my old green Philippine passport all by myself. At 21, I braved life as an OFW in Taiwan—learning early on that bureaucracy loves paperwork more than Filipinos love a good *chismis*.

Fast forward to today: even with a US Green Card in hand, I’ve learned firsthand that holding foreign status doesn't magically exempt you from the endless loops of travel documentation. I built Mamae Knows Borders because I’ve stood in those confusing lines and paid those unnecessary fees so you don't have to walk in blind. Hindi madali, pero kaya natin!
