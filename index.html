Bakit ko binuo ang site na ito? (Why I Built This)

I grew up in a small town in Bulacan, Philippines, carrying a big childhood dream: seeing the magical landscapes of "Middle Earth" and traveling across different countries.

At 20 years old, I processed my old green Philippine passport all by myself—traveling all the way to Manila and arriving at 3:00 AM just to secure a slot. At 21, I experienced firsthand the struggle of leaving as an OFW to Taiwan, dealing with high processing fees, agency anxiety, and the overwhelming paperwork required of a young Filipino stepping abroad for the first time.

Years later, even now as a permanent resident in New York holding a US Green Card, I know firsthand that holding foreign status doesn't completely exempt us from the complexities of travel documentation and visa anxiety. I built this site because I deeply understand the friction, financial stress, and confusion that Filipinos face when trying to cross borders—whether for a quick vacation, a job contract, or immigration. Hindi madali, pero kaya natin.
