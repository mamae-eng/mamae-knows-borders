Passport & ID Last Updated: October 2026

Simple Guide to Getting Your PH Passport

  1. Get a Valid ID First: Secure your PhilSys National ID, UMID, or Postal ID.
  2. Book Online: Go to the official DFA booking portal. Never pay fixers on social media!
  3. Prepare PSA Docs: Bring your original PSA Birth Certificate.
  4. Show Up Early: Arrive 30 minutes before your appointment with printed forms and receipts.
Visit DFA Booking Portal ⚠️ Rules subject to change
Visa-Free Travel Last Updated: October 2026

ASEAN Visa-Free Checklist

  1. Passport Validity: Must be valid for at least 6 months before your trip.
  2. Return Ticket: Confirmed return or onward flight ticket is required.
  3. Hotel Booking: Print your hotel reservations or host details.
  4. Employment Proof: Bring your company ID or certificate of employment just in case.

Bulacan to Global Borders

Hi, I’m Mamae!

I grew up in a small town in Bulacan, Philippines, dreaming of Middle-earth and collecting passport stamps until my passport begged for mercy.

At 20, I survived the legendary 3:00 AM Manila stampede just to process my good old green Philippine passport—all on my own. By 21, I was already navigating life as an OFW (Overseas Filipino Worker), learning one important lesson early: bureaucracy loves paperwork almost as much as Filipinos love a good serving of adobo and lumpia.

That’s why I created Mamae Knows Borders. To share what I've learned, simplify the complicated, and help fellow travelers navigate life across borders.

Hindi madali, pero kaya natin! Let’s navigate the world one passport stamp at a time.
