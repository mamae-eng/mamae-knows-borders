About Mamae

Hi, I’m Mamae!

I grew up in a small town in Bulacan, Philippines, dreaming of Middle-earth and collecting passport stamps until my passport begged for mercy.

At 20, I survived the legendary 3:00 AM Manila stampede just to process my good old green Philippine passport—all on my own. By 21, I was already navigating life as an OFW, learning one important lesson early: bureaucracy loves paperwork almost as much as Filipinos love a good serving of adobo and lumpia.

That’s why I created Mamae Knows Borders. To share what I've learned, simplify the complicated, and help fellow travelers navigate life across borders.

Hindi madali, pero kaya natin! Let’s navigate the world one passport stamp at a time.🌍✨
