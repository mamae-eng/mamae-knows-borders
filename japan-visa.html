Japan Travel

Japan Tourist Visa Guide for Filipinos

Last Updated: October 2026

Complete walkthrough for Japanese Temporary Visitor visas, core documents, financial proof, JVAC submission, and approval tips.

🇯🇵 Visa Type: Temporary Visitor (Tourism) 💵 Visa Fee: FREE (Standard service/agency fees apply) 📍 Submission: Via JVAC (VFS Global) or Accredited Agencies

Detailed Guide & Sections

Essential Application Documents

  • Valid Philippine Passport: Must have two blank pages and be in good condition.
  • Visa Application Form: Completely and accurately filled out (typed or handwritten legibly without erasures).
  • Passport Photo: Recent 4.5cm x 3.5cm photo with white background attached to the form.
  • PSA Civil Documents: Original PSA Birth Certificate and Marriage Certificate (if applicable).
  • Schedule of Stay: Day-by-day itinerary of your activities in Japan.

Mamae's Real-Talk Tip:

Huwag gumamit ng friction pen o pambura sa Japan visa application form! Kailangan pulido at walang bura ang bawat detalye, o ire-reject agad sa counter.
